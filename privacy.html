Privacy Policy

No data collected. No tracking. No sharing.

This page explains how Tiger Coin Quest handles privacy. The short version: the app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

What we do (and do not do)

Clear & Transparent

Tiger Coin Quest is designed to work without collecting information about you.

  • We do not collect personal data (name, email, identifiers, etc.).
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs that send data to third parties.
  • We do not store user profiles on servers.
  • We do not sell or share user data.

If the app stores any preferences, progress, or settings, they stay on your device only.
