FAQ

Quick answers
Is there a subscription or in-app purchase?

Not yet. At the moment, there are no paid features or subscriptions. In the future, monetization may be added to support development (details will be clearly shown in the app and store listing).

Can you delete my data?

There is no account system and no server-side user data. If anything is stored, it’s local to your device.

Where can I see what’s new?

Updates are published through the App Store release notes. You can also email me to ask what’s planned next.
